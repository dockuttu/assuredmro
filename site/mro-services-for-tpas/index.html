What TPAs get

  • Graduated volume pricing. The TPA / Lab plan is $199 a month with 40 results included, then $5.50 per result for results 41–500, $4.50 for 501–2,000 and $3.75 above 2,000 each month.
  • One price, positive or negative. Donor interviews and Clearinghouse reporting are included, so non-negatives don’t blow up your cost per test.
  • DOT and non-DOT on one account. FMCSA, FTA, FAA, FRA, PHMSA and USCG programs, plus non-DOT employer programs with custom panels.
  • Clearinghouse reporting handled. We report MRO-required entries for your CDL drivers so you don’t have to follow up.
  • Month to month. No setup fee and no long-term contract. Ask about annual pricing at higher volumes.

Who reviews your results

Results are reviewed by Robin Arora, MD, a physician certified as an MRO by the American Association of Medical Review Officers (AAMRO) through August 2027 and board-certified in internal medicine, nephrology and addiction medicine. Under 49 CFR 40.121, a qualified MRO can review DOT results for covered employees in every state, so one MRO relationship covers your whole client list.

FAQ

Common questions

What does MRO review cost for a TPA?

The TPA / Lab plan is $199 a month with 40 results included. Beyond that, results 41 to 500 are $5.50 each, 501 to 2,000 are $4.50 and anything over 2,000 is $3.75, each month.

Do positive results cost more?

No. The per-result price covers negative and non-negative results, including the donor interview and Clearinghouse reporting.

Can you serve our clients in multiple states?

Yes for DOT testing, where a qualified MRO can review results for covered employees in every state. For non-DOT programs we follow each client’s policy and note any state-specific requirements at setup.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No. Plans are month to month with no setup fee. Higher-volume TPAs can ask about annual pricing.

How quickly can we start?

Most TPAs are routing results to us within a few days of choosing a plan, once the laboratory lists Assured MRO as the MRO of record.
