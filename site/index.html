What we do

Complete MRO review, from lab result to verified report

Every result gets the same process under 49 CFR Part 40 for DOT programs, and the same standard for non-DOT programs.

Result verification

We review every laboratory result and custody and control form. Negatives are released quickly. Non-negative, adulterated, substituted and invalid results go through full MRO review.

Donor interviews

The MRO personally contacts each donor with a non-negative result. We verify any legitimate medical explanation, such as a valid prescription, before a result is finalized.

Clearinghouse reporting

We report verified positive and refusal results for FMCSA-regulated drivers to the Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse, as the MRO is required to.

Split specimen coordination

When a donor requests a split specimen test, we coordinate it with a second certified lab and handle the paperwork.

Medical evaluations

We review medical evaluations for shy-bladder and insufficient-breath situations, and handle safety-concern and fitness-for-duty determinations.

Secure reporting

Verified results are reported confidentially to your designated employer representative. Complete records are available for audits and litigation.

Credentials

A physician MRO, certified to federal standards

Under DOT rules (49 CFR 40.121), a licensed physician with MRO training and certification from an HHS-approved board may act as MRO for covered employees wherever they are located. Assured MRO's medical review officer is certified by the American Association of Medical Review Officers (AAMRO), one of the certifying organizations approved by HHS.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you serve donors in any state?

Yes, for DOT-regulated testing. Federal rules let a licensed, certified MRO review results for covered employees wherever they are located. Some states have their own rules for non-DOT workplace testing, and we'll confirm any state-specific requirements for your program when we set you up.

Do you charge more for positive results?

No. Your per-result price covers negative and non-negative results alike, including the donor interview and Clearinghouse reporting. Only optional services such as split specimen coordination are priced separately.

How does the monthly minimum work?

Your plan's monthly minimum counts toward your testing. For example, Employer Essentials is $49 a month and includes your first 5 results. You only pay more if you go over the included results.

How fast are results reported?

Negative results are typically released within one business day of the lab reporting them. For non-negative results, the MRO starts review the day the lab reports and contacts the donor promptly. Timing then depends on reaching the donor and getting any documentation.

Do we need to change labs or collection sites?

Usually not. We work with SAMHSA-certified labs and your existing collection network. You just designate Assured MRO as your MRO.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month with no setup fee. Volume customers can ask for annual pricing.
